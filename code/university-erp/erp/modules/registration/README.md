# registration · Course Registration (class module)

**Owner office:** Registry / Academic Affairs · **Built by:** the whole class in weekly labs · **Stakeholder persona:** the Deputy Registrar (Academic)

Each semester, students register the courses for their level and programme within the credit-load limits set by the
Senate, their course adviser approves, and the HOD signs off. Registration is the hub of the ERP: fees, examinations,
timetabling and the library all depend on knowing who is registered for what.

Weekly labs build it together: week 3 interviews the stakeholder, week 4 writes the spec, week 5 builds Feature 1 in
parallel worktrees, week 6 mutation-tests the credit-load rules, week 7 threat-models it, week 9 integrates it with
the team modules through events.
